# Starting Locations — Meridian Bay, alternate modern Earth

These locations were chosen for the fictional scenario after the owner delegated the starting region. They are established **opening-setting facts**, not a complete global map.

## CITY-MERIDIAN-BAY — Meridian Bay
A modern coastal port city with a working harbor, mixed commerce, public transportation and residential areas. The calendar year and nation remain intentionally unspecified. No existing country or real person is implied.

## LOC-CROSSWATER-PLAZA — Crosswater Transit Plaza
A heavily used evening meeting point: glass-fronted terminal entrance, pedestrian square, route information boards, benches, a few food and retail storefronts, and the harbor visible between buildings. Jack's opening scene starts here. No device has been placed or data stolen as of the initial checkpoint.

## LOC-TRANSIT-TERMINAL — Crosswater Terminal
Bus and rail connections adjacent to the plaza. Lena works with transit operations. Its normal delays, maintenance and passengers create independent activity.

## LOC-COMMERCIAL-DISTRICT — Market Row
Small businesses, food vendors, offices and delivery traffic within walking distance; Darius has an ordinary commercial delivery to make.

## LOC-HARBOR-WALK — Harbor Walk
Public walkway toward the waterfront with views of industrial and passenger port activity.

## Coarse connectivity
Crosswater Plaza connects to Crosswater Terminal, Market Row and Harbor Walk. Market Row also connects to Harbor Walk. Detailed routes, distances and private areas are not yet established; do not invent travel times as fixed facts.

## Before Day 0
No superhuman activity, wave-caused transformation or omniscient knowledge of the distant shockwave. Normal local problems and independent lives should carry the opening story.


## LOC-RAILGARDEN-RESIDENTIAL — Railgarden
Barrio residencial de Meridian Bay, con edificios modestos y comercio cotidiano. Conecta con Market Row y el hogar Wilson.

## LOC-WILSON-APARTMENT — Hogar de los Wilson
Apartamento funcional compartido por Jack, Elena y Rosa. Su situación de propiedad o alquiler no está decidida. El hogar cubre sus necesidades normales y no exhibe las reservas privadas de Jack.

## LOC-WILSON-WORKSHOP — Taller de Jack
Espacio dentro de la vivienda para reparaciones, herramientas, gadgets y **Proyecto Aster**, que todavía está incompleto. No confundir sus dispositivos almacenados aquí con el estuche que lleva en la plaza.
