# World canon — approved decisions

Only the following are established world facts. Proposed details in chapter drafts or examples do not become canon automatically.

- **Theme:** Living Superhuman World, an original interactive novel (NOT an RPG). Heroes, villains, civilians, animals and organizations have independent goals; the world does not wait for the protagonist.
- **Opening:** Play begins on Earth at **Day -30**, thirty in-world days before the first superhumans appear.
- **Day 0:** A mysterious cosmic shockwave originating from a distant corner of the universe reaches Earth and leads to superhuman emergence.
- **Awakening:** Some humans gain abilities immediately, some much later, and some never.
- **Scope:** Humans, animals, plants and the environment can be affected.
- **Morality:** Powers do not determine who is a hero or villain. Actions, motivations and consequences matter.
- **Delivery:** ChatGPT narrates a free-form interactive novel in the conversation. The repository supplies its persistent world reference, chapters and state; there is no RPG system of levels, XP, ranks or numerical character stats. No separate download is required.
- **Setting style:** An alternate version of modern Earth, with original places and institutions (owner approved).
- **Repository governance:** Public reading is acceptable. Repository writes must use existing permissions and owner-controlled review; no automatic merge is authorized.

## Not yet established

The shockwave's true origin and mechanism; exact transformations and awakening frequencies; the calendar year; geography and institutions outside the established opening location; future factions and story outcomes. Jack Wilson and the opening Meridian Bay scenario are tracked in saved story state, not as universal world rules. Do not invent these as settled canon.

## Canon updates

New owner-approved lore belongs here and in `docs/DECISIONS.md`. A player's in-world actions become *story history* when resolved, not general universe rules. Never retcon established history silently.
