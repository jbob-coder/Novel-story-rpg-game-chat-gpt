# World canon — owner-approved

This file records established setting decisions, **not speculative lore**. Implementation tuning in code is provisional unless explicitly approved.

- Genre/theme: **Living Superhuman World** — an original, evolving world whose heroes, villains, civilians, and organizations pursue independent goals, with or without the player.
- Story begins **one month (30 in-game days) before** the first superhumans appear.
- A **cosmic shockwave from a distant corner of the universe** reaches Earth on Day 0 and causes the emergence.
- Awakening is **hybrid**: some humans awaken immediately, others days/months/years later, and some never.
- The shockwave affects **humans, animals, plants, and the environment**.
- A person's powers do **not** automatically define them as hero or villain; motivations, circumstances, and choices matter.
- The world is not dependent on the protagonist for its events to progress.

## Still undecided

- The shockwave's true origin, physical mechanism, intentionality, and propagation details.
- Exact proportion of affected people, ability taxonomy, power balance, and biological/ecological rules.
- Geography, political setting, protagonist identity and background, final game name, engine, platform, and interface format.
- Whether environmental and nonhuman changes are beneficial, harmful, or mixed.
