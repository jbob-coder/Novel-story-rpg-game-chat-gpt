# Historic RPG progression rules — RETIRED (D-036)

**Not an active rulebook.** The owner replaced the RPG with a free-form **interactive novel**. No numerical levels, XP, skill ranks, attributes, class systems, dice or progression rewards may be created, awarded, computed or shown. Resolve abilities through established backstory, actual practice, circumstance, preparation and consequences. Characters can learn and change in natural prose.

Past story events and `game/STATE.json.player.progression` contain historic RPG numbers. These remain **frozen for archival and checkpoint compatibility** until a separately validated schema migration. Do not increase or reinterpret them; their existence is not an instruction to use an RPG. The old numerical thresholds and balancing proposals are available only in Git history.

For active rules see [NARRATIVE_CONTRACT.md](NARRATIVE_CONTRACT.md), [SCENE_CRAFT.md](SCENE_CRAFT.md) and [NARRATOR_RULES.md](NARRATOR_RULES.md).
